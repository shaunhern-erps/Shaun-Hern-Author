About Shaun

Written from the Porch. Not the Mountaintop.

Shaun Hern is a 61-year-old single dad to two neurodivergent kids, a faith-based memoirist, and the voice behind the GrandpaDad brand. He writes from the place most people are actually living — where faith is real but not always tidy.
