Why this matters more than it sounds like it should

Reporting gaps are one of the most common — and most fixable — issues I run into. Most companies aren't missing data. They're missing a reporting layer that makes the data usable without a manual rebuild every time someone needs a number.

Few ERP consultancies go deep on both the reporting layer and the platform underneath it — that combination is most of the point of working with PinPoint instead of a generalist.
