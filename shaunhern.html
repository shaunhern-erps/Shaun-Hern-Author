About Shaun

A Dad. A Grandpa. A Voice for Families Who Keep Going.

Shaun Hern didn't set out to become an author. He set out to be present — for his children, his family, and the faith that anchors everything. Parenting later in life, walking alongside special needs caregivers, and trying to center a messy, complicated world on Christ gave him something far more valuable than a platform: a story worth telling.


Under his GrandpaDad brand, Shaun writes and speaks with an authenticity that resonates deeply in communities that rarely see themselves on the page — grandparents raising grandchildren, older fathers figuring it out, families navigating disability, and everyday believers trying to live with faith as their compass.
