About Shaun

Written from the Porch. Not the Mountaintop.

Shaun Hern is a 61-year-old single dad to two neurodivergent kids, a faith-based memoirist, and the voice behind the GrandpaDad brand. He writes from the place most people are actually living — where faith is real but not always tidy, and belief shows up tired some days. His books are less about having it figured out and more about keeping company with people who are still trying.


He believes deeply and still gets distracted. He trusts God and still argues with Him occasionally. He knows Scripture and still forgets to live it out before coffee. That honesty is the whole point. Shaun writes about his nonverbal daughter Ll'annelee, his endlessly verbal toddler son Logann, and the daily business of parenting children who need everything you have — turning flour footprints, fridge padlocks, and mop-bucket spa days into something that looks a lot like grace.


In his devotional and memoir work, Shaun brings Scripture down to floor level — pairing Psalm 121 with a late-night worry spiral, 2 Corinthians 12 with a meeting where nobody listened, Galatians 6 with the quiet of carrying something too long. His aim is simple: to sit beside the reader. To remind them that God doesn't need us impressive. He needs us available.
